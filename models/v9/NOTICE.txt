Modifications by RoGuard
  The Romanian and Ukrainian classifier checkpoints are fine-tuned derivatives
  of the listed encoders. The field heads and decision combiners were trained
  for RoGuard. The interpreter adapter contains RoGuard-trained low-rank
  parameters for the listed Gemma base; the downloaded upstream base files are
  unchanged. Model-selection records, hashes and limitations are recorded in
  research/v9-stabilization and docs/V9_STABILIZATION.md.
