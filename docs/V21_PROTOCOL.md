# V21 prospective Romanian NLI-adapted D1 study

V20's four-output mmBERT encoder failed on the new batch 0037 at 18/48 complete D1 pairs. Its two test phrasings caused opposite errors: overreview on one and missed positives on the other. A post-test exploration of the already consumed batches 0034, 0036, and 0037 found that neither a simple V16/V18 score combiner nor a stricter V16 cutoff transfers consistently. The next registered change is a different **linguistic representation**: adapt an existing multilingual natural-language-inference (NLI) encoder to answer four separate Romanian abstract-field hypotheses, then apply the unchanged typed D1 rule. No real or realistic child message, case paraphrase, or proposed reply enters this study.

The base is [`MoritzLaurer/mDeBERTa-v3-base-xnli-multilingual-nli-2mil7`](https://huggingface.co/MoritzLaurer/mDeBERTa-v3-base-xnli-multilingual-nli-2mil7), MIT-licensed at revision `b5113eb38ab63efdd7f280f8c144ea8b13f978ce`. Its model card reports multilingual NLI training that includes Ukrainian but not Romanian; the training corpus was machine-translated and the card cautions about translation quality. This experiment tests Romanian abstract-card transfer; the source card does **not** establish RoGuard's D1 accuracy, Ukrainian taxonomy quality, or child-message validity. Pin the six local base-file SHA-256 values in `src/roguard/nli_v21.py` and reject a mismatched base.

## Frozen data and method

Use the attested D1 portions of batches 0028, 0031, 0032, 0033, 0034, and 0036 as **training**: 528 cards in 264 one-fact negative-to-positive pairs. All have been consumed by earlier experiments. Use batch 0037 as **development only**: 96 cards in 48 pairs on two previously consumed phrasings. Its prior test result cannot be reused as a fresh V21 claim. Batch 0038 is the new sealed test: 96 abstract cards in 48 pairs, split between two further invented Romanian phrasings. Its generator, semantic rule, and this protocol are committed before training. Do not generate, open, or score batch 0038 until the selected development adapter and its scores are committed.

The model reads each card as an NLI premise paired with each of the four fixed Romanian hypotheses in `src/roguard/nli_v21.py`: minor source, safety/support anchor, indirect/repeated support pattern, and explicit support request. A positive field is an entailment target (`0`), an absent field a contradiction target (`2`); neutral (`1`) remains a model output but is never a training target. The field score is the sigmoid of the entailment logit minus the contradiction logit, with cutoff `0.5`. Apply `minor AND anchor AND (indirect OR explicit)` without a learned rule or new cutoff.

Fine-tune rank-16 LoRA on `query_proj` and `value_proj` (alpha 32, dropout 0.05), saving the trainable NLI classifier and pooler alongside the adapter. Use seed `20261003`, maximum length 256, three epochs, two complete pairs per batch, AdamW learning rate `3e-5`, 10% warmup and linear decay. For each of the eight card–hypothesis tasks in a pair, optimize 3-class cross-entropy weighted by the inverse frequency of its positive/negative field state **from training only**. Add `0.25` times a unit-margin loss requiring the changed field's entailment-minus-contradiction logit to increase from negative to positive, and `0.05` times the mean absolute probability change of invariant fields. Use no test-derived data, threshold, or checkpoint choice.

Save every epoch adapter and all raw development scores. Select by complete development pairs, then fewer false reviews on negatives, then correct field judgments, then earlier epoch. The development gate is **at least 42/48 complete pairs**, **at least 42/48 D1 positives recovered**, **at most 6/48 negative false reviews**, and **at least 340/384 field judgments correct**. If it fails, keep the failure and selected diagnostic checkpoint; do not relax the gate. Commit the selection, adapter, development scores, exact weight/config hashes, and this code before generating batch 0038.

At test, score batch 0038 once with the frozen V21 adapter and unchanged V16, V18, and V20 adapters. Report exact cards and pairs, D1 TP/FP/FN/TN, false reviews, all four field confusion counts, parse validity, raw scores, and exact input hashes. V21 passes this **synthetic** target only if it reaches **at least 42/48 complete pairs**, **at least 42/48 positives recovered**, **at most 6/48 false reviews**, and exceeds each frozen comparator in complete pairs on the same test. A failure remains in Git. Even a pass would support only transfer among invented Romanian abstract descriptions; independent language review and an ethically appropriate evaluation population remain necessary before a real child-language claim.
