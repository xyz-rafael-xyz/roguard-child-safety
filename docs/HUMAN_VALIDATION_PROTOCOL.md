# Prospective bilingual taxonomy and annotation validation

**Status: ready for independent reviewers; no review or annotation result has been collected.** This protocol concerns the Romanian and Ukrainian taxonomies and permitted abstract cards. It cannot validate recognition of a child's authentic disclosure under RoGuard's rule against storing real or realistic disclosure text.

The [reviewer handoff](REVIEWER_HANDOFF.md) gives role criteria, possible institutional introduction routes, an invitation draft, and a concrete transfer checklist. No outreach or review has occurred.

## 1. Freeze the question before review

Record the exact taxonomy digest for each language and the six category codes. The [Romanian review record](../taxonomy/review_ro_independent.json) and [Ukrainian record](../taxonomy/review_uk.json) remain pending. Two **different people** per language should inspect the entire taxonomy independently: one fluent language reviewer and one child-safety reviewer who can also assess its language. Use the [Romanian](RO_REVIEW_PACKET.md) or [Ukrainian](UK_REVIEW_PACKET.md) packet. Check the role terms *tutore* and *піклувальник* specifically against the [terminology audit](ROLE_TERMINOLOGY_AUDIT.md). Do not treat a machine-generated record or two signatures from the same person as independent review.

Record category decisions and proposed edits in the review records only after the reviewers return their own judgments. If wording changes, freeze a new digest, obtain reviews of the new bytes, and decide whether existing synthetic labels or adapters must be regenerated or retired. The software validates record structure and hashes; a study owner must verify identities, qualifications, independence, and the substance of corrections.

## 2. Build a separate annotation set

After the taxonomy is frozen, create **new, language-native** Romanian and Ukrainian abstract D1/S1 cards. Do not translate a Romanian card set into Ukrainian. Use pattern and response-field descriptions, one-fact counterfactual pairs, harmless hard negatives, and a declared `source_kind`. Balance each outside author's pairs across every D1 fact or S1 response field as specified in the [intake contract](INDEPENDENT_BATCH_INTAKE.md). Include no actual or plausible child utterance, case paraphrase, grooming dialogue, or proposed reply. Preserve exact card bytes and opaque IDs before annotation. The study owner checks that every card is in scope and that the D1 source role, safety/support anchor, indirect/repeated pattern, and explicit request can be judged from the abstract description without guessing.

The [independent batch intake](INDEPENDENT_BATCH_INTAKE.md) now admits a new test batch from two outside authors after a separate content review and independent taxonomy approval. Its provenance record binds each pair to an author and marks labels as author intended, **not adjudicated**. Automated shape and hash checks cannot verify the authors' real identities, independence, language competence, or the content reviewer's judgment. `roguard-pairs` refuses to score those intended labels; the human evaluation path uses separate adjudications.

Keep the model, its prompts, threshold, and any training data separate from this prospective set. Prior batches 0004–0038 have informed model development or frozen comparisons and cannot serve as a fresh test of the next tuned model. A new set may be used for **human rubric reliability** first; if the same items later become a model test, freeze the model before revealing adjudicated labels or scores, and report that the test is on abstract descriptions only.

## 3. Collect independent judgments

Give two fluent reviewers per language the same items in independently randomized orders, with opaque shared item IDs. `roguard-blind-packets --batch batch-0035 --category D1 --output-dir review_runs/pilot-0035` demonstrates a local packet builder for an attested abstract batch; use a newly frozen study batch for a prospective validation. Give each reviewer only their own packet, never `owner-map.json` or repository access to the reference labels. The packet builder removes source IDs and labels and uses different row orders; it cannot verify that the reviewers actually work independently. Blind each reviewer to the generator's reference label, the other's answers, and model scores. `roguard-annotate-packet review_runs/reviewer-a.jsonl review_runs/reviewer-a-answers.jsonl` presents each permitted abstract card and collects only numbered D1/S1 judgments, saving completed rows so work can resume after interruption. The single-card `roguard-review-card --language ro --item-id item_opaque --compact` (or `uk`) remains available. Unknown D1 facts remain `null`; unknown S1 applicability or pass makes the support decision incomplete. Store each reviewer's JSONL stream separately under appropriate access control. Do not put reviewer names or underlying material in the annotation stream or repository.

Use `roguard-agreement-batch` to align streams by ID and report per-category complete-pair decision agreement, pass/review confusion counts, exact fact matches, incomplete rows, and Cohen's kappa. Review all disagreements and incomplete items while **blind to model output**. Preserve both original judgments and a separate adjudicated decision; do not overwrite a reviewer's file to make agreement look higher. The tool does not verify independence, source-card alignment, language quality, or label truth. Cohen's nominal-scale statistic is useful alongside the full confusion matrix, but its interpretation depends on the annotation task and class mix; report counts and prevalence rather than treating a single kappa cutoff as certification. This choice follows [Cohen's original agreement coefficient](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/001316446002000104) and the [computational-linguistics agreement review](https://aclanthology.org/J08-4004/).

When using the blinded packet builder, run `roguard-review-audit` with the owner packet directory and the two original answer streams in reviewer A/B order. It verifies exact packet bytes, source-card alignment, and each answer stream's exact ID order against its assigned packet before computing agreement. A swapped or reordered answer file fails. The audit still cannot verify who annotated the cards, whether they worked independently, or whether their answers are right. Keep `owner-map.json` away from reviewers throughout annotation.

## 4. Evaluate and state the supported claim

The numeric target for a **new independently authored abstract D1 or S1 batch** is fixed before any such batch exists: at least 44/48 pairs must retain the intended negative-to-positive contrast **and differ on exactly the declared factor** after independent adjudication, with at least 22/24 valid one-fact pairs from each author and at least 10/12 for each D1 factor or 5/6 for each S1 factor. Model parse coverage must be 96/96; recall, specificity, and precision on complete adjudications must each be at least 0.90; exact rate on valid one-fact pairs must be at least 0.90 overall, 0.85 in each author's surface, and 0.80 for every factor. Status-only contrasts with a different fact, multiple facts, or no fact changed remain in the report but cannot count toward this target. `roguard-human-eval` reports `inconclusive_insufficient_valid_pairs` when the human valid-pair minimum fails, `misses_numeric_target` when adequate cards fail any model criterion, or `meets_numeric_target` when all criteria hold. These are demanding **abstract-task** criteria, not deployment thresholds. The report keeps human independence, adjudication validity, and real child-language accuracy unverified even for a numeric pass. Generated batches have no applicable prospective target.

For a model frozen before the new set is opened, report Romanian and Ukrainian separately: exact one-fact pairs, D1 and S1 TP/FP/FN/TN, precision and recall with denominators, false reviews on negative cards, parse validity, and results for the always-review and never-review references. `roguard-human-eval` binds a frozen prediction stream to the exact owner packet, two original streams, and a separate adjudication stream. It excludes ambiguous adjudications from confusion counts, counts model parse failures as abstentions, and shows whether reviewers rejected the generator's intended contrast. It cannot establish that a model was frozen before labels were seen, that the reviewers were independent, or that the adjudication was right; record those facts outside the command. A failed result remains in Git, and the next change needs another prospective test.

The evaluator reports author and factor surfaces without publishing author IDs and gives [95% Wilson intervals](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm) for recall, specificity, precision, raw contrast-pair rate, and valid one-fact pair rate. These intervals are descriptive: paired cards and shared authors violate the independence assumption of a simple binomial interval, so their lower bounds cannot certify real-world performance. Preserve the raw counts and adjudication attrition alongside every interval.

`roguard-predict-abstract` consumes only the shuffled `reviewer-a.jsonl` packet for a **Romanian** D1 or S1 batch and writes opaque-ID, packet-hash-bound decisions from a pinned local mmBERT research adapter and its manifest cutoff. Keep the source batch, provenance, owner map, and judgments away from the model operator. The predictor rejects malformed and Ukrainian packets and overwrite attempts; it cannot establish the packet's origin or human approval. `roguard-human-eval` later checks that the exact packet came from an attested independent batch and rejects source-ordered prediction files for such batches. Run inference only after recording the model freeze and before opening adjudicated judgments. The current Ukrainian training and inference gate remains closed pending fluent review and a separate model study.

The frozen model studies V15–V21 show large changes across invented Romanian wording, including opposite false-review and miss patterns across V20's two new surfaces and V21's 9/48 complete-pair NLI result. Research on synthetic-data faithfulness documents how a generated language distribution can differ from the real distribution; a higher score on more generated cards cannot bridge that gap by itself ([Veselovsky et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15041)). Even excellent independent agreement on abstract cards would support the **abstract taxonomy and annotation task**, not sensitivity to real child messages, Ukrainian model accuracy, age-based comprehension, or an operational reporting rule. The safe operational claim remains limited to the read-only checker of caller-declared contracts until a separately justified real-world evaluation can be designed without violating the project's data rule.
