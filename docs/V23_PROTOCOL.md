# V23 Romanian D1 group-weighted development study

V22 balanced exposure to seven consumed Romanian abstract-card families reduced false reviews but lost positive reviews: its selected epoch reached 73/96 complete pairs, 75/96 positives, and 3/96 false reviews on four consumed development surfaces. Unchanged V16 reached 75/96, 85/96, and 13/96. V23 tests whether emphasizing training families with higher loss while modestly weighting positive cards can preserve the gain in specificity without the large recall loss. This is a **development-only** experiment. Batches 0037 and 0038 have already informed the hypothesis and cannot become a new held-out test.

## Fixed inputs and training recipe

Start from the unchanged V16 Romanian mmBERT adapter, weight SHA-256 `9f3ccab9b75b9aad5b42739518348bc3af84fd8037d17f3b49ecdb741a858f1d`, the same pinned base revision, D1 prompt, maximum length 256, and decision cutoff `0.5`. Use only approved D1 message pairs from consumed training batches 0028, 0031, 0032, 0033, 0034, 0035, and 0036. Never fit on 0037 or 0038. For each of three epochs, draw 24 distinct pairs per training family without replacement using seed `20261005` plus the epoch index, shuffle the 168 pairs, and train two pairs per optimizer step. This keeps exposure balanced before reweighting. No new cards, child utterances, or realistic disclosures are generated.

Use AdamW at `2e-5`, 10% warmup and linear decay over 252 optimizer steps. Each pair contributes mean two-class cross entropy, with positive-class weight `1.5`, plus `0.5` times a softplus penalty when its positive logit margin does not exceed its negative margin. Maintain one nonnegative group weight per training batch, initialized uniformly. After each two-pair minibatch, multiply each observed family's weight by `exp(0.05 × min(pair loss, 4))`, normalize all seven weights, then average that minibatch's pair losses using their current group weights. Keep weights across epochs. This online group reweighting is a training intervention; it never reads development labels when updating weights.

Commit this protocol and [trainer](../training/train_mmbert_v23.py) before fitting. The trainer refuses a dirty worktree or existing output directory, verifies the exact V16 source, approved batch structure, and saved V16 comparator, and records input hashes, environment versions, epoch weights, per-family training loss, checkpoint hashes, and raw development scores.

## Development choice and interpretation

Evaluate every epoch on the four 24-pair surfaces in consumed batches 0037 and 0038, separately. Select by highest worst-surface complete pairs, then highest total complete pairs, then fewest false reviews, then earliest epoch. Keep the V22 gate unchanged: at least 18/24 complete pairs on **each** surface, at least 84/96 overall, at least 90/96 positives, at most 8/96 false reviews, and more than unchanged V16's 75/96 complete pairs. Preserve every checkpoint and failure. A passing development result would nominate a candidate for a separately frozen, independently authored and adjudicated abstract test; it would not establish authentic child-language accuracy. A failed result remains a failed result regardless of later reruns.
