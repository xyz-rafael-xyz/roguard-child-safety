# RoGuard 10.0 reel

The project and profile READMEs embed a **silent, infinitely looping GIF** made from the supplied `roguard_reel_27s.mp4`. Its filename says “27s”, but the actual video is **45.47 seconds** long. The GIF keeps all 1,364 source frames in order at 640 × 360, with the same 45.46-second playback duration and average **30 frames per second**. GIF timing is limited to 10-millisecond steps, so its individual frames alternate between 30 and 40 milliseconds. The source MP4 remains unchanged in Downloads.

[Looping GIF](../media/roguard-v10-reel-mp4-loop.gif)

## What the reel shows

The reel moves from a large RoGuard 10.0 title into a diagram of the reused Gemma 4 body, two feature views, four Romanian/Ukrainian RBF heads and the guarded decision rule. A Romanian newspaper example illustrates how reference scope can change a review call. A chart then compares **33 combined v9 binary and concern output errors with zero v10 output errors** on the same 160 project-authored synthetic cases: the 120-case policy-corrected panel plus the 40-case challenge O. The visual is a designed explanation, not a screen recording or a new evaluation.

The 33 errors are **output counts**, not necessarily distinct messages: v9 made 17 binary and 16 concern errors across those cases. V10 made zero in those two outputs on that finite authored sample. The underlying [public-wheel record](../research/v10-evidence/stable-release-20261110/publication.json), [panel result](../research/v10-evidence/stable-release-20261110/public-wheel-panel20b-result.json), [challenge result](../research/v10-evidence/stable-release-20261110/public-wheel-challenge-o-result.json) and [precise v9/v10 comparison](V10_VS_V9.md) explain the result and its limits. These checks do not establish perfect accuracy on real children's messages or independent human validation. A review proposal does not verify an event or authorize external action.
