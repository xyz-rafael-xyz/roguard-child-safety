# V19 prospective joint D1 field study

V18's separately prompted factor encoder failed at 32/72 complete D1/S1 pairs on batch 0034, with 31/48 D1 positives recovered and 22/48 D1 negatives falsely reviewed. Full inference replay reproduced every saved score. V19 tests a different representation for **abstract Romanian D1 cards only**: one four-output encoder reads each card once, predicts the minor-source, support-anchor, indirect-pattern, and explicit-request fields jointly, and applies the unchanged rule `minor AND anchor AND (indirect OR explicit)`. This is not a child-message detector.

Training uses the already consumed, attested D1 portions of batches 0028, 0031, 0032, 0033, and 0034: 432 cards or 216 adjacent one-fact pairs. Old test cards become *training* data in this new study and cannot be claimed as new evidence. New [batch 0035](../data/synthetic/batch-0035.preview.md) is development: 24 D1 pairs on one new abstract surface. The independently generated batch 0036 is the sealed diagnostic test: 48 D1 pairs split across two further surfaces. Its generator and all thresholds are frozen before training, but its rows are not generated or scored until the selected development checkpoint is committed. The cards state invented fields only, never a child's utterance, case paraphrase, or proposed reply. Automated attestation verifies exact generator reproduction, one-fact labels, and lexical separation; it cannot establish authentic-language validity.

Initialize `jhu-clsp/mmBERT-base` at revision `c5955035435e2bf121cde7f3c8863ef52ff35d82` with a newly trained four-output classification head and rank-16 LoRA on `Wqkv` and `Wo` (alpha 32, dropout 0.05). Use seed `20261001`, maximum 256 tokens, three epochs, AdamW at `5e-5`, batches of four pairs, 10% warmup, linear decay. Each step receives both cards from the same pair. Optimize mean four-field binary cross-entropy plus `0.25` times a unit-margin penalty requiring the changed field's logit to increase from negative to positive, plus `0.05` times the mean absolute probability change on the three invariant fields. Every pair must differ in exactly one typed field. No test-derived threshold or prompt tuning is allowed; field cutoff is fixed at 0.5.

After each epoch, save the adapter and all batch-0035 raw field scores. Choose the epoch by complete D1 pairs, then fewer false reviews on negatives, then correct field judgments, then earlier epoch. The development gate requires at least **21/24 complete pairs**, at most **3/24 false reviews**, and at least **170/192 correct field judgments**. Even if the gate fails, package the selected checkpoint for one diagnostic test, preserving the failure. Commit the selected adapter, hashes, all development predictions, and selection record **before** generating or opening batch 0036.

At test, compare V19 with unchanged V16's direct D1 decision and V18's four-prompt factor decision on the same 96 batch-0036 cards. Record exact pairs/cards, D1 TP/FP/FN/TN, false reviews, four-field accuracy, all raw score vectors, parse validity, and input hashes. V19 passes the registered synthetic target only if it reaches **42/48 complete pairs**, recovers **42/48 D1 positives**, flags at most **6/48 D1 negatives**, and exceeds both frozen comparators in complete pairs. A failure stays in Git; future changes need another fresh test. Passing would establish only stronger transfer among invented Romanian state descriptions. It would not establish authentic disclosure recall, Ukrainian accuracy, or readiness for live screening.
