# Methodology and provenance

1. Review the six-category Romanian v0.2 taxonomy and its [research note](TAXONOMY_RESEARCH.md), including the provisional `D1` boundary, `R1` policy boundary, permission changes and repaired-boundary recurrence, human gate, and age-band language. Rafael approved v0.2 on 29 September 2026; the exact content digest is in `taxonomy/review_ro.json`. A separate [Ukrainian draft](../taxonomy/taxonomy_uk.md) now exists, but Ukrainian model training and claims remain disabled pending independent language and subject-matter review.
2. Generate a candidate batch from fixed abstract templates. Every row has a source type, language, labels, split, and template origin. The registered generators read no NeurIPS dataset or case log.
3. Validate each new generated batch automatically. The validator regenerates all rows from its registered source, checks exact labels and preview rows, binds the batch, generator, audit, and taxonomy hashes, checks class coverage and exact text overlap, and records each row ID. A changed input invalidates the attestation. This is a reproducibility gate, not proof that synthetic language reflects real children. Earlier human approvals remain in their historical records; Rafael removed the per-batch human review requirement after batch 0007.
4. Training and evaluation ingestion accept only explicitly named, attested batches. They verify exact bytes, unique IDs, source type, labels, taxonomy, and split separation. A test batch cannot enter training.
5. Freeze held-out synthetic test batches before fitting. Report counts, model revision, adapter settings, prompt, strict parse, precision and recall per category and language. Mark unavailable measurements explicitly. Once a test is inspected, do not reuse it to support a revised model claim.

The first two generated batches used a superseded seven-category draft and are archived. Rafael approved the Romanian v0.1 taxonomy and its 12-row batch 0003 on 29 September 2026; both are archived byte-for-byte without training. Rafael subsequently approved the v0.2 taxonomy and all 28 rows of batch 0004, then separately approved batches 0005–0007 on the same date. Batch 0004 is a **training-split boundary probe**, too small and narrow to support a model result by itself. None of these cards establishes that a classifier can recognize a child's real disclosure. Later batches require independently authored, language-specific surface variants and automated provenance checks. Synthetic templates cannot estimate real-world prevalence, calibration, or performance on actual children. No real disclosure, chat log, or paraphrase may enter any split.

`screen()` returns scores and signals. `calibrate()` derives thresholds on a validated development split only. It rejects missing or nonfinite scores, labels incompatible with a card's source kind, and categories without both positive and negative development cards. A threshold meeting an empirical recall target on those cards does not guarantee that recall on a new population. `assess_contracts()` checks declared structured facts without a model. `assemble_report()` marks model `D1/A1/S1` output as advisory and `R1/P1/G1` output as unverified policy hints; it never replaces the deterministic policy checks. `escalate()` returns a suggestion for a human reviewer. None of these functions sends a notification, report, or external action. A real deployment would require domain review and separate evaluation.

`python -m roguard` exposes the declared-contract checks as a local JSON command. It accepts `ro` and `uk` symbolic cards because the rule checker operates on explicitly supplied fields; its Ukrainian output names the taxonomy as a draft. It does not invoke the Romanian model or assert language understanding.

The taxonomy is conditional on `source_kind`: `D1` applies to a declared minor message; `R1`, `P1`, and `G1` apply to structured decision cards; `A1` and `S1` apply to responses. A complete routing, permission, or gate card admits a deterministic reference decision. A model trained on abstract cards is at most a research baseline for those cards, not a replacement for the reference rule. Evaluation must report wrong-principal, unauthorized-use, over-withhold, target-field attribution, false blocks, and review load separately where applicable. No overall “safe” label follows from an empty label set.

The current JSONL schema stores category labels, not the taxonomy's evidence bands, subcodes, or review priorities. Batch 0004 is for inspecting category boundaries only. It is not a training or validation set for the finer decisions. A later annotation schema must preserve those distinctions before claiming calibrated routing or a subcode-level benchmark.

The pure `check_readability_contract`, `check_routing`, `check_permission`, `check_boundary`, and `check_gate` functions are reference checks for **caller supplied declared contracts**. The additional `check_disclosure_evidence` and `check_support_contract` functions evaluate caller-reviewed D1/S1 field judgments, without reading or validating the underlying message or response. They return reason codes. They cannot establish legal authority, notify anyone, prove the content of a real message, or validate a Romanian age-to-reading-level scale. The learned model is evaluated separately on linguistic card classification; agreement with a reference check on templated cards is not evidence of real-world disclosure recognition.

The local `roguard-review-card` command collects D1 judgments through numeric choices, preserving unknown values as `null`. For a blinded abstract-card annotation study, two streams can be matched by opaque item IDs and audited with `roguard-agreement-batch`. It reports exact fact agreement, incomplete rows, and a pass/review confusion matrix for each category. Decision agreement and Cohen's kappa use only pairs where both reviewers supplied complete facts; incomplete counts remain visible separately. Kappa is undefined when the expected agreement denominator is zero and is reported as `null`. The audit cannot verify that the item IDs refer to the intended cards, that reviewers worked independently, or that matching labels are correct. No human annotation study has yet supplied evidence for Romanian or Ukrainian language validity; the demonstration streams are invented flag records only.

Batches 0005–0007 are human-approved pilot material with 60 training, 40 development, and 40 test descriptors. Their decision rules intentionally overlap the training cards while wording and symbolic values differ. This tests whether a model can handle the narrow abstract-card representation. It is not an independent sample from real Romanian conversations. Row order is mixed, and the card text contains no category letter or ordered row number as a label cue. The MLX path emits hard category codes, so its 0/1 API values are not calibrated probabilities; malformed outputs are recorded as abstentions and count against positive recall.

The first model produced a source-kind shortcut on batch 0007, which was inspected once and frozen in [the benchmark](../BENCHMARK.md). That observation informed a revised prompt; batch 0007 therefore cannot be reused as an independent test for the revision. The new prompt and its adapter were assessed on batch 0006, then on the newly [validated batch 0008](../data/synthetic/batch-0008.review.json). Batch 0008 was opened once for a fixed comparison with the base model. The adapter made 23/40 exact matches versus the base model's 25/40; this is a measured failure of the fine-tuning claim, not an invitation to optimize on batch 0008. The [full run record](../eval/runs/ro-v2-test-0008-manifest.json) binds the two results to the same test bytes.

The later [one-factor diagnostic](../docs/CONTRAST_PROTOCOL.md) was frozen before running the v2 adapter and two prompt-only bases. It revealed weak pair consistency and is now consumed. A separate [v3 protocol](V3_PROTOCOL.md) registers a larger paired training batch 0009, development batch 0010, and untouched test batch 0011. Their surface frames differ, but the same underlying rules recur. The automated validator reports this limitation rather than treating character-distance checks as semantic independence.

The v3 test was opened once for the [fixed six-model comparison](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-paired-card-comparison-batch-0011). The adapter collapsed toward `NONE`; most prompt-only models over-flagged negative cards. Batch 0011 is consumed. The separately frozen [Ukrainian prompt-only probe](UK_PROBE_PROTOCOL.md) used Ukrainian symbolic cards under an unreviewed draft taxonomy and also failed pair consistency. It is not a trained Ukrainian model result.

The [v4 protocol](V4_PROTOCOL.md) responded to the Romanian collapse with a category-specific binary task, balanced one-token target labels, a development-only checkpoint choice, and a new test batch 0012 sealed before fitting. Its automated attestation proves exact reproduction and split separation, while the task remains narrow and template based. The aborted uppercase-label preflight and its tokenizer finding are recorded in that protocol. The [development selection](../eval/runs/ro-v4-dev-selection.json) chose step 500 and was committed before batch 0012 inference. The [comparison record](../eval/runs/ro-v4-test-0012-comparison.json) binds its exact weight, train and development exports, prompt, test, and predictions. V4 remained weak on complete pairs and missed three whole categories; a lower false-review load did not make it safe to use.

The [v5 protocol](V5_PROTOCOL.md) froze a score-bearing readout of the same adapter and a new 48-pair batch 0013 before calibration or test inference. The [development threshold record](../eval/runs/ro-v5-dev-thresholds.json) was committed before test opening; the [frozen comparison](../eval/runs/ro-v5-test-0013-comparison.json) records its failure on five categories. Batch 0013 is consumed. The separate `roguard-pairs` command can audit any model's predictions against a registered abstract pair batch without trusting labels supplied by the prediction file. It reports pair consistency and false review load in addition to per-category counts. It remains an abstract benchmark tool, not validation on real child language.
