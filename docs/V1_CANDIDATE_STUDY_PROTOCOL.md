# Corrected v1 candidate abstract-card study

This protocol uses the exact Romanian and Ukrainian candidate taxonomy digests accepted in the owner-attested review records. It is separate from the historical v0.2/v0.1 author kit and V25 study freezes. The study concerns invented descriptions of annotation fields only. It cannot measure accuracy on authentic child messages or authorize automatic notification, reporting, or disclosure.

The implementation is included here for inspection and blind annotation. The candidate freezes, model weights, blank author packets, and future private judgments are in the private research archive. This public checkout cannot verify a private model freeze or produce a v1 score without those inputs. The `roguard-v1-annotate` command can be used with a label-free packet supplied privately by the study owner; no packet is ready until authors and the content reviewer finish.

## Roles and separation

For each language, the owner has designated two authors (`RO-A1`, `RO-A2` or `UK-A1`, `UK-A2`), two blind annotators (`RO-N1`, `RO-N2` or `UK-N1`, `UK-N2`), a content reviewer (`RO-C` or `UK-C`), and an adjudicator (`RO-J` or `UK-J`). Names and contacts stay outside Git. The owner verifies identity, language competence, independence from model work, and any cross-role overlap. The software verifies only opaque IDs, declarations, hashes, and file shape. Authors must not annotate or adjudicate their own cards. Each language's content reviewer is distinct from its authors. Annotators do not receive intended labels, the owner map, or model scores. Adjudicators resolve disagreements without model output.

## Frozen question

The four cells are `ro:D1`, `ro:S1`, `uk:D1`, and `uk:S1`. Each cell has 48 one-fact pairs, 24 per author, for 96 cards. D1 assigns the five corrected fields `source_role`, `safety_or_support_anchor`, `indirect_or_repeated_support_pattern`, `explicit_support_request`, and `direct_safety_statement`. S1 assigns the eight historical response fields plus `immediate_danger_route` and `no_pressing_for_details`. The two authors receive balanced but separately shuffled assignments. A later retraction can be recorded in D1 annotation but cannot erase a previously supported direct or indirect signal. S1's two added fields are assessed only when a card makes them applicable.

Before author tasks are issued, commit a cell-specific JSON freeze under `eval/prospective/`. It must pass `verify_v1_study_freeze` and bind: the approved candidate taxonomy and review record, model weights and manifest, the prompt and fixed cutoff, the predictor, all v1 author/packet/annotation/evaluation modules, and the numeric target. The freeze names an earlier Git commit whose bytes match the current checkout. An older V25 freeze, by itself, does not pass this candidate binding. Do not send blank assignments until that exact freeze is committed and pushed. Record the author-kit hashes in a content-free issuance record before authors write. Human timing and remote receipt remain owner-attested.

## Card creation and safety review

Authors write descriptions of fictional fields in their assigned language. Each pair has an intended negative and positive differing on the assigned factor only. Authors do not copy earlier RoGuard cards or translate another author's cards. No real or realistic child utterance, case paraphrase, grooming dialogue, or proposed support reply is permitted. The script checks one-line shape, writing system, factor allocation, exact duplicates, hashes, and lexical overlap warnings. A fluent content reviewer inspects every card and the overlap report, then signs an exact-hash declaration. Automated checks cannot establish semantic one-fact validity or native-language quality.

`python -m roguard.v1_study_kit` creates private JSON workbooks and TXT instructions. `python -m roguard.v1_study_packet prepare` creates a private content-review candidate and overlap report. `python -m roguard.v1_study_packet seal` creates two differently ordered, label-free JSONL packets only after the distinct content reviewer approves exact bytes. The owner keeps `OWNER-MAP.json`, `SOURCE-CARDS.jsonl`, workbooks, and intended polarities away from annotators and the model operator. `python -m roguard.v1_study_annotate` saves each annotator's structured answers separately; no card text is copied into answer files.

## Blind prediction, adjudication, and score

The model operator receives only `reviewer-a.jsonl`, the pinned model, and the registered predictor command. The predictor writes packet-hash-bound opaque-ID decisions with strict parse flags. Commit and push a content-free seal of the owner-map and packet hashes before model prediction. Commit and push the frozen prediction before intended labels or adjudicated answers are opened or published. Preserve both original annotator streams. A separate adjudicator returns a third stream while blind to model predictions. `python -m roguard.v1_study_eval` then recomputes card and pair metrics from exact files. It rejects swapped packet IDs, changed card text, changed author workbooks, changed content-review bytes, and a model ID unlike the freeze. These checks cannot prove that the named model actually ran or that people followed the blinding order; retain a private handoff record and Git chronology.

The numeric target is fixed before independent authoring: at least 44/48 adjudicated valid one-fact pairs; at least 22/24 valid pairs per author; at least 8 valid pairs per D1 factor or 3 per S1 factor; 96/96 parsed predictions; and at least 0.90 precision, recall, specificity, and exact rate on valid one-fact pairs. Exact rate must also be at least 0.85 for each author's surface and 0.80 for every factor. A cell with too few valid pairs is inconclusive. An adequately adjudicated cell that misses any model criterion is a failed model result. A failed result stays in the record; changes to the model require a new untouched prospective set. Report counts and denominators, not just a pass/fail flag.

Four passing abstract cells would support only the measured abstract task. The public declared-contract checker remains the usable read-only path. No v1 release should describe the research text model as validated on real child language.
