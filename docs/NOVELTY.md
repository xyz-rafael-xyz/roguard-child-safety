# What RoGuard can contribute

**Research position as of 30 September 2026.** RoGuard combines a child-focused decision taxonomy, Romanian abstract-card model evaluation, a separately authored Ukrainian taxonomy draft, one-factor contrast tests, and a read-only checker for declared routing, permission, boundary, accessibility, and workflow contracts. The useful distinction is between *language interpretation* and *authority over information*. A classifier may suggest a signal; a model code cannot create a guardian's permission or trigger an external action.

| Work | Reported focus | Relevant limit for RoGuard |
|---|---|---|
| [Llama Guard 4](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama/Llama-Guard-4-12B) | Broad MLCommons-style input/output safety categories | Its model card does not report Romanian or Ukrainian among its listed multilingual test languages. Its categories do not encode RoGuard's declared permission-state checks. |
| [YouthSafe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08997) | Youth-focused taxonomy and safeguard model | Already invalidates a generic “first child-safety classifier” claim. RoGuard must test its distinct routing and state boundaries, not rename existing youth risks. |
| [KORA](https://github.com/korabench/benchmark) | Child-facing conversation benchmark with age groups, risks, and mechanisms | Already invalidates a generic “first child-safety benchmark” claim. Its public description does not establish a Romanian/Ukrainian declared-contract toolkit. |
| [CAREBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29685) and [KIDBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25510) | Child risk and development-sensitive evaluation | These motivate response and age checks; their reported results cannot be imported as RoGuard accuracy. |
| [Ukrainian Guardrails](https://huggingface.co/mark-matviiv/ukrainian-guardrails) | Ukrainian safety encoder with a national-security taxonomy | Already invalidates a generic “first Ukrainian guardrail” claim. Its published card lists different labels. |
| [RoSafetyBench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/rosafety-bench/rosafety-bench) | Romanian LLM safety benchmark | Already invalidates a generic “first Romanian safety benchmark” claim. RoGuard's child-specific decision scope still needs direct comparison. |

The first version's measurable contribution is a **counterfactual, language-specific evaluation protocol plus a typed, fail-closed contract checker**. The [Romanian contrast result](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-one-factor-diagnostic-contrast-0001) shows why one-factor pairs matter: aggregate exact-match counts hid models that kept returning the same category when a decisive condition changed. The [Ukrainian probe](UK_PROBE_PROTOCOL.md) is exploratory until a fluent and child-safety reviewer checks the taxonomy. No claim of high accuracy on authentic child language follows from symbolic cards.

The later [v5 test](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-token-margin-comparison-batch-0013) sharpened this distinction: a threshold choice that reached 11/18 exact development pairs fell to 2/48 on a fresh symbolic surface. The project now exposes `roguard-pairs` as a model-agnostic, hash-bound contrast evaluator and [audits the typed permission-state machine](CONTRACT_AUDIT.md) independently. These are concrete tooling contributions. The token-margin classifier itself failed its preregistered criterion and should not be described as the project's successful guardrail.

The [v6 compositional study](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-compositional-comparison-batch-0016) made this failure harder to dismiss as a small-data artifact: 576 reference-checked training cards and a 144-card fresh test still yielded only 9/72 exact pairs, below the prior adapter's 14/72. It missed every positive P1 card. The novel, currently working part is the separation of evidence ownership and exact structured checks, plus a benchmark that exposes such category failures rather than smoothing them into an aggregate accuracy claim.

The [v7 transfer study](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-qwen-transfer-comparison-batch-0017) exposed a different, measurable failure: a Qwen adapter learned an empty chat-template wrapper around its binary answer, so a literal output contract parsed 0/144 held-out cards. Its 72 exact negatives would look like 50% card accuracy if parsing were hidden. RoGuard records parsing, one-fact pair behavior, and false reviews separately, preserving the cause of failure. The [v8 prospective amendment](V8_PROTOCOL.md) registers a narrow decoder repair and a development-matched comparator before the next sealed test. This is an evaluation and engineering contribution, not evidence of child-safety detection ability.

The [v8 fresh comparison](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-balanced-qwen-comparison-batch-0018) confirms that output-format validity and semantic discrimination are separate. The amended decoder parsed all 144 cards, yet category-balanced training reached only 13/72 complete pairs, below the original-exposure adapter's 15/72, and missed all S1 positives. A measured failure is useful here: the toolkit now exposes training exposure, strict parsing, one-fact consistency, and false-review load as distinct, hash-bound dimensions instead of collapsing them into a single accuracy number.

The typed interface now covers all six category codes with separate evidence ownership: the caller supplies reviewed D1 pattern and S1 response-field judgments, while the software checks their declared completeness and field-level outcome; routing, permission, and gate facts have their own deterministic checks. The read-only `roguard-contrast` command additionally verifies that two valid contracts differ in exactly one scalar fact and shows which decision changed without repeating the input values. This separation and counterfactual audit are testable architecture claims, not proof that the software understands Romanian or Ukrainian child language.

The [v13 focused study](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-d1s1-advisory-transfer-batch-0025) passed its preregistered held-out target: 47/48 complete pairs for abstract D1/S1 descriptions with unchanged v11 weights and no false review on 48 negatives. It supports a **narrow advisory research role** for that encoder while exact declared-contract checks handle the policy questions. It cannot establish authentic child-language accuracy; the broader six-category text classifier still fails its targets. The contribution is the measured division of labor and the evaluation protocol, not a generic “first guardrail” claim.

The [v14 frozen study](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-mixed-input-workflow-batch-0026) tested that division of labor on a new, sealed six-category symbolic batch. The combined workflow got 72/72 complete pairs, while the same frozen text-only adapter got 55/72 and missed all A1 positives. The improvement comes with **more information**: R1/A1/P1/G1 receive typed generated facts. The novel, inspectable contribution is an evidence-aware interface and counterfactual test that shows exactly which decisions require declared authority, permission, or numeric facts, and which still rely on language interpretation. This is not a like-for-like classifier gain or a live child-safety validation.

The working name **RoGuard** collides with an [unrelated Roblox monitoring product](https://roguard.org/). The GitHub repository uses the scoped slug `roguard-child-safety`, and package registration remains undecided. A publication should describe the exact measured contribution and cite prior child-safety and language-specific work rather than claim generic priority.
