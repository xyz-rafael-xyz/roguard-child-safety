# V22 Romanian D1 worst-surface development study

The unchanged V16 direct classifier ranked the positive card above its one-fact negative in 185/192 pairs across eight consumed abstract surfaces, but its fixed cutoff produced only 138/192 complete pairs. The worst surface reached 4/24. A retrospective search on all consumed scores proved that no **single** cutoff for unchanged V16 can lift the worst surface above 7/24. V21's multilingual NLI adaptation failed more severely. V22 tests a representation change rather than another cutoff: continue V16 with equal exposure to seven previously consumed Romanian surface families and select the epoch by the worst of four separate development surfaces. This study has **no new test set or held-out accuracy claim** until independently authored, ethically permitted cards are available.

## Frozen inputs and fit

The source is the private V16 selected mmBERT LoRA adapter, weight SHA-256 `9f3ccab9b75b9aad5b42739518348bc3af84fd8037d17f3b49ecdb741a858f1d`, with pinned JHU mmBERT-base revision `c5955035435e2bf121cde7f3c8863ef52ff35d82`, its original Romanian D1 prompt, and its unchanged score cutoff of `0.5`. Train only on approved D1 message pairs from consumed batches 0028, 0031, 0032, 0033, 0034, 0035, and 0036. For every one of three epochs, draw 24 distinct pairs from each batch without replacement using seed `20261004` plus the epoch index. Batches with exactly 24 pairs contribute all pairs. This gives 168 pair exposures per epoch and prevents the largest generator family from dominating. No new child message, realistic disclosure, or response text is used.

Use two pairs per optimizer step, maximum length 256, AdamW learning rate `2e-5`, 10% warmup and linear decay. Continue the rank-16 V16 adapter and trainable classifier head with binary cross-entropy and `0.5` times the softplus pair-order loss. Keep the prompt, label order, LoRA configuration, and `0.5` cutoff fixed. Save each epoch adapter, training loss, and raw development scores. Require a clean Git commit containing this protocol and trainer before fitting.

Use consumed batches 0037 and 0038 as **development only**; each contains two 24-pair surfaces. They have already informed the diagnosis and cannot serve as fresh tests. Evaluate all four surfaces separately. Select the epoch by: (1) highest worst-surface complete pairs, (2) highest total complete pairs, (3) fewest false reviews across 96 negatives, then (4) earliest epoch. The development gate is at least 18/24 complete pairs on **every** surface, at least 84/96 overall, at least 90/96 D1 positives recovered, and at most 8/96 false reviews. The unchanged V16 comparator has 75/96 complete pairs, 85/96 positives, 13/96 false reviews, and a 10/24 worst surface across the same four development surfaces. V22 must also beat its 75/96 total. Preserve every failed checkpoint and score record; do not loosen the gate after looking.

The selected adapter is a **development candidate only**, even if it passes. The next valid comparison requires two fluent, independent Romanian authors/reviewers to supply new abstract, pattern-level cards after the model, prompt, cutoff, and evaluation code are frozen. No real or realistic disclosure/grooming content may enter that study. Blind double annotation and separate adjudication should establish that the invented descriptions actually carry the intended labels before scoring. A parallel Ukrainian model remains gated on independent review of its draft taxonomy. A development improvement or a synthetic future test cannot establish accuracy on authentic child language.
