# RoGuard research model card

**Scope:** Romanian symbolic-card classification research and a separate Ukrainian draft-taxonomy probe. The supported, model-free product path is the read-only [declared-contract checker](CLI.md), which accepts caller-supplied facts in Romanian or Ukrainian. Neither path is validated for interpreting an authentic child's message. No path reports, notifies, changes access, or executes an external action.

## Inputs and decisions

The experimental **Romanian** adapters consume invented card descriptions and return category scores or codes. Some MLX studies ask a category-specific `da`/`nu` question; the mmBERT and NLI studies score encoder heads instead. Hard 0/1 codes and softmax scores are **not calibrated child-safety probabilities** and do not establish identity, permission, legal authority, urgency, or safety. `screen()` exposes codes, and `escalate()` returns only a suggestion for human review. `assemble_report()` keeps any model label separate from findings derived from declared contracts. A model's R1, P1, or G1 label never authorizes information sharing or an external workflow. The Ukrainian Qwen3 run is a separate prompt-only research probe, not an adapter offered by `screen()`.

The structured checker verifies complete fictional routing, permission, boundary, gate, support-field, disclosure-pattern, and declared word-cap facts. Its result is only as sound as the caller's facts and policy. Missing facts remain `incomplete`; a guardian role does not receive access by default. D1 and S1 structured fields are reviewer judgments supplied by the caller, not text interpretation by this package.

## Data and models

Training and evaluation data are original, abstract descriptions generated from typed reference states. They contain no real or realistic child disclosure, grooming exchange, child utterance, or assistant response. Adjacent negative/positive pairs change one fact. Split membership, generator bytes, labels, preview, taxonomy hash, and validation records are tracked in Git. This distribution is deliberately balanced and artificial; its prevalence and wording do not match a deployment population.

The Romanian RoMistral adapters use the pinned [RoMistral-7b-Instruct](https://huggingface.co/OpenLLM-Ro/RoMistral-7b-Instruct) base, local 4-bit MLX conversion, and rank-16 LoRA. The v6 checkpoint was selected on batch 0015 before its fresh batch-0016 test. The [Qwen3 study](V7_PROTOCOL.md) used the pinned [Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507) 4-bit MLX snapshot and a new batch-0017 holdout. The [mmBERT v9a study](V9A_AMENDMENT.md) used the hash-pinned [JHU mmBERT-base](https://huggingface.co/jhu-clsp/mmBERT-base) encoder and a pairwise LoRA fit; its new batch-0019 test also failed the accuracy target. Seven selected MLX, nine mmBERT, one mmBERT-based NLI, and one [Romanian BERT](V24_PROTOCOL.md) adapter are retained in the private Git repo as research artifacts. The Romanian BERT candidate failed its development gate on consumed cards and has no new test. Base weights and other training intermediates remain local under ignored `checkpoints/`. The repository's MIT software license does not relicense any base or adapter weights. Check each upstream model card before any further distribution.

## Measured limits

The [frozen v6 comparison](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-compositional-comparison-batch-0016) found 9/72 exact pairs and 39/72 false reviews on negative cards. The [v7 transfer comparison](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-qwen-transfer-comparison-batch-0017) found that its trained Qwen adapter parsed 0/144 cards under the literal output contract. The [v8 comparison](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-balanced-qwen-comparison-batch-0018) repaired parsing to 144/144 but found only 13/72 exact pairs. The [v9a encoder comparison](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-pair-trained-encoder-comparison-batch-0019) reached 28/72 pairs with 26/72 false reviews; [v10](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-cross-category-encoder-comparison-batch-0020) reached 28/72 with 40/72 false reviews. The [v11 surface comparison](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-surface-generalization-comparison-batch-0023) reached 47/72 pairs and missed all 12 A1 positives, below unchanged v10's 53/72 pairs on that same sample. [V12 category thresholds](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-category-threshold-comparison-batch-0024) fell from 60/72 development pairs to 46/72 fresh-test pairs, below the frozen v11 cutoff's 56/72 on the same scores. These six-category studies failed their preregistered symbolic-card targets. A narrower, unchanged-weight [v13 D1/S1 advisory test](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-d1s1-advisory-transfer-batch-0025) passed its registered **symbolic** target with 47/48 complete pairs and zero false reviews. The [v14 mixed-input study](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-romanian-mixed-input-workflow-batch-0026) then passed at 72/72 pairs, but four categories received generated typed facts unavailable to the text-only model, which got 55/72; this verifies the combined symbolic workflow, not model extraction of those facts. Later V15–V21 studies each failed their registered fresh-test targets on changed abstract wording; [V22](V22_PROTOCOL.md), [V23](V23_PROTOCOL.md), and [V24](V24_PROTOCOL.md) failed development-only targets on consumed surfaces and have no new test. A separate Ukrainian prompt-only Qwen3 probe matched 0/24 exact pairs under a separately written but **unreviewed** Ukrainian taxonomy. A [grammar correction diagnostic](UK_NUMERAL_DIAGNOSTIC.md) left all eight A1 decisions unchanged. A later [single-rule binary prompt diagnostic](UK_BINARY_PROMPT_DIAGNOSTIC.md) fixed parse format across four models but got at most 5/24 pairs on the same consumed cards. These measurements cannot be read as real-child detection accuracy, Ukrainian classifier quality, comprehension by children, or operational safety.

For practical use, collect an explicit, complete contract and let a human verify the facts and any proposed response. The current models are for research diagnostics on abstract cards only. Before any authentic-language detection claim, the project needs independent fluent Romanian and Ukrainian review, child-safety subject review, a governed evaluation source compatible with the no-real-disclosure rule, and evidence across prevalence, age, and wording shifts. No such validation is claimed here.

An additional [frozen benign-request probe](../BENCHMARK.md#frozen-benign-romanian-request-probe-neutral-0001) gave the unchanged v11 adapter 0/48 false D1 reviews on original Romanian homework, creative, and general-information requests with a declared minor-role prefix. This negative-only, out-of-domain result does not test disclosure recall or establish real-message specificity; always abstaining would pass it.

Its [score comparison](../eval/analyze_mmbert_d1_shift.py) found a descriptive shift in negative D1 scores between abstract cards and those benign requests, despite identical weights and cutoff. The softmax values are uncalibrated; abstention on a small benign set does not establish a stable decision margin for live use.
