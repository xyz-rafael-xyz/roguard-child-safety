# Frozen Romanian category-specific binary study, v4

This protocol and [test generator](../training/generate_binary_challenge.py) are committed before fitting or opening batch 0012. The [registered validation record](../data/synthetic/batch-0012.review.json) binds 48 symbolic held-out cards in 24 one-fact pairs. Its maximum same-kind character similarity to earlier approved batches is 0.629; overlapping policy logic remains. No card contains child speech or a realistic assistant response.

The v3 adapter collapsed to almost-always `NONE` on both development and held-out cards. The v4 intervention changes only the output task: ask separately whether each applicable category requires review, with a strict `da`/`nu` answer. Response cards produce separate A1 and S1 decisions. The 288 batch-0009 training cards yield 480 category tasks. A1 and S1 positives are repeated three times in the **training export only**, giving 240 `da` and 240 `nu` targets; the 36 batch-0010 development cards yield 48 unrepeated tasks. Repetition is a limited balancing device, not additional independent evidence. The [prompt](../src/roguard/prompt_v4.py) and output mapping are fixed here.
