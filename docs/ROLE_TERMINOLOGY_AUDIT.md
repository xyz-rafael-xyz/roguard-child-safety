# Guardian-role terminology audit, 30 September 2026

This note identifies a language review issue. It is **not** independent Romanian or Ukrainian approval, a legal interpretation for any deployment, or a change to the frozen taxonomy bytes.

The current Romanian R1 title uses *tutore*, and the Ukrainian draft uses *піклувальник*. Both are familiar words, but they also name specific legal roles. The [Romanian Civil Code, Article 143](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/FormaPrintabila/00000G11EADACKVBD3X3D62X3XBB4PGX) uses *tutore* for legal representation of a minor in defined circumstances. [Romanian UNICEF child-rights material](https://www.unicef.org/romania/media/2271/file/Conven%C5%A3ia%20Na%C5%A3iunilor%20Unite%20cu%20privire%20la%20Drepturile%20Copilului.pdf) distinguishes parents from a *tutore* who takes responsibility when a child has no parents. In Ukraine, the [Family Code terminology register](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/term/18843) distinguishes *опіка* for a child under 14 from *піклування* for ages 14–18. [UNICEF Ukraine's care guide](https://www.unicef.org/ukraine/support-parenting) also uses the broader phrase *особи, які здійснюють догляд* for people providing care. These sources support a **risk of unintended legal or age-specific meaning** in a generic product role. They do not choose the correct product term for RoGuard.

The taxonomies already state that their guardian-like roles are fictional policy labels and establish no legal relationship. The checker also grants no recipient access from a role name alone: the caller must supply a matching scope for the principal, item, purpose, and recipient. A [bilingual role-label regression test](../tests/test_role_terms.py) checks this with neutral Romanian and Ukrainian labels. It verifies program behavior, not the quality of the words.

## Decision for independent reviewers

For each language, decide whether the taxonomy should use the existing legally loaded word, a broader care-role phrase, or a neutral product-role label. Check the title, definition, out-of-scope statement, and examples together. In particular, ask whether a reader could infer that **all** parents or caregivers have access, or that the software established legal guardianship. Record the preferred term and reasoning in the language's [Romanian](RO_REVIEW_PACKET.md) or [Ukrainian](UK_REVIEW_PACKET.md) review packet. Any adopted wording change will be a new taxonomy version with a new hash; previous model measurements remain tied to their original text.
