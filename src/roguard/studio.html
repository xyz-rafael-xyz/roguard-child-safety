Scope of this tool
These checks use caller-supplied fictional facts and the historical six-category contract rules. The corrected v1 D1/R1/S1 checks are available through the separate CLI. A passing result does not verify a child's words, a person's authority, a real permission, or language quality. No report, notification, or account change is sent.
