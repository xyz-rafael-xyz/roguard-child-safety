# Code of Conduct

RoGuard is a child-safety research toolkit. Its community should be a place where people can challenge ideas, code, translations, and evidence without attacking the people doing the work.

## Scope

This code applies to maintainers and contributors in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-run spaces. It also applies when someone represents RoGuard in a public discussion or event.

## How we work together

- Treat people with respect across languages, backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience.
- Make criticism specific to a claim or contribution. Explain what evidence would change your view, and correct your own mistakes openly.
- Give Romanian and Ukrainian language questions the care they deserve. State uncertainty instead of presenting a translation or model output as expert judgment.
- Respect contributors' privacy. Do not press anyone to reveal a reviewer, child, family, or reporter's identity.
- Keep examples abstract and synthetic, as required by [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). This repository is not a channel for case reports or child disclosures.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized attention, intimidation, repeated unwanted contact, and retaliation against someone who raises a concern are not acceptable. Neither are publishing private information without consent; submitting real or realistic child disclosures, grooming text, or case details; or deliberately misrepresenting review, credentials, or evaluation results. Do not quote or mirror harmful material in a reply, even to object to it.

Good-faith disagreement about taxonomy, safeguards, model failures, or project decisions is welcome. Maintainers may still ask for evidence, close off-topic threads, or reject a change on technical grounds.

## Reporting a concern

For a project concern you can describe without sensitive details, [open an issue](https://github.com/xyz-rafael-xyz/roguard-child-safety/issues/new) and link the relevant public thread. If it needs a private reply, use the issue only to request a private contact route; do not describe the incident there. The project does not currently publish a private maintainer inbox.

For abuse in a GitHub issue, pull request, or comment, use **Report content → Report abuse to GitHub Support** on that content. This is a private report to GitHub, not a message to RoGuard's maintainer. Do not post child data, realistic disclosure examples, or someone's private information in any report to this repository.

If the concern involves the maintainer, use GitHub's [abuse reporting route](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) rather than asking the subject of the report to handle it. This project cannot promise independent adjudication by a second maintainer.

## How reports are handled

The maintainer will assess concerns received by the project in context, limit access to sensitive information, and avoid asking a reporter to confront the person involved. Where practicable, the person reported will have a chance to respond. Depending on severity and recurrence, the maintainer may edit or remove content, ask for a change in behavior, warn someone, lock a conversation, or restrict participation. Deliberate privacy breaches or threats may justify immediate removal or restriction. Retaliation for reporting is itself a violation.

The maintainer will share an outcome with the reporter when a private reply route exists, while protecting other participants' privacy. No confidentiality or response-time guarantee can be made for reports sent through a third-party platform. GitHub may take separate action under its own policies.

This code governs project participation; it does not provide case handling, emergency response, legal advice, or a way to send a child-safety report through RoGuard.
